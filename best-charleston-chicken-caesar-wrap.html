The Best Chicken Caesar Wrap in Charleston, SC

When it comes to easy, satisfying Charleston lunch wraps, the chicken caesar wrap at Sweet Palm Coffee on King Street is the one locals keep coming back to. Fresh, filling, and dependable — here's why it's become one of the best chicken sandwich alternatives in downtown Charleston.

A Local Favorite Among Charleston Wraps

On a food blog like Chow Down Charleston, there's always something new to try, but every now and then a simple menu item leaves a bigger impression than expected. That's exactly how the chicken caesar wrap at Sweet Palm Coffee feels. Among the many Charleston wraps and King Street wraps worth trying, this one keeps rising to the top of the list.

Sweet Palm Coffee may be known to many people for coffee and espresso drinks, but this wrap deserves attention all on its own. The chicken caesar wrap has the kind of flavor that makes you think about ordering it again before you even leave. The chicken is hearty enough to make it feel like a real Charleston chicken sandwich alternative, while the caesar profile gives it that familiar, savory taste that works so well for lunch. Wrapped together into something convenient and easy to eat, it checks all the boxes for downtown workers, students, and locals on the go.
